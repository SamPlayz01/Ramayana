These are the screens you will use, drawn from the app itself. The tabs across the top are Notes, Tasks, Org, Analytics, Rewards and Leaderboard.

Organize people into groups and sub-groups

The Org tab is where the organization account builds its structure.

  • Groups nest. Add a sub-group to any group, add members, or remove them with one click.
  • Add existing or create new. Put current accounts in a group, or make new accounts and assign them immediately.
  • Group admins. Make someone an admin of a group or sub-group so they can run it without owning the whole organization.
  • Accounts are named under your organization, like [email protected].
  • Manage passwords. Re-roll a password, delete one account, or select several and delete them together.

Assign a task with a real deadline

Every task goes to one group, and everyone in it receives it.

  • Exact due time, not just a day. It defaults to 11:59 PM today.
  • Reference links and files go with the task, up to 25 MB per file.
  • Whole group or specific people. People who join the group later do not receive it.
  • Standard or Automated. An automated task is marked by AI against your checklist, see below.
  • Deadlines are enforced. After the due time, unsubmitted tasks close.

  • Drill down from the whole organization to a group, a person or a single task, with breadcrumbs back.
  • Needs attention lists people with missed deadlines. Search finds any person, group or task.
  • Include sub-groups rolls child groups into a parent. Download Excel exports the current view.
  • History and growth. Save snapshots of a team's results, then compare past groups with the current one.

Let AI mark the work against your checklist

Create an Automated Task, write what to check for, and set a pass mark.

  • You write the checklist: a short marking scheme with marks for each point.
  • You set the pass mark, from 50% to 100%.
  • Good work is accepted on the spot. The rest goes back with feedback and a one-tap Fix and resubmit.
  • Attempts to sway the AI are flagged and sent back. Only the owner creates automated tasks.

Papers turn finished work into a streak

Finish a task and get it checked and you earn a paper. Let one close unfinished and you lose one.

  • Counted from the real task record, so it cannot be faked.
  • The Rewards tab shows what you earned and lost. Organizations see every member side by side.
  • Organizations can take papers back, with a note the member can read.
  • The Leaderboard ranks public profiles by papers, kings or verified work. Tap a row to see someone's verified work and badges.

How it's built

Plain web technology, chosen to stay quick on school laptops and phones.

Vanilla JavaScript

No front-end framework. The dashboard is a single page of ES modules, so there is nothing heavy to download or hydrate.

Supabase

Postgres and Edge Functions power sign-in, organizations, tasks, notes sync and file upload and download.

IndexedDB

Notes live in your browser first, with a sync queue and an automatic sync every five seconds.

SheetJS

Reads .xlsx, .xls and .csv imports and writes your Excel exports, all inside the browser.

Flat and solid

Pure white panels, or solid charcoal in dark mode. No blur, sheen or glow, so it stays steady on slower devices.

DM Serif Display and DM Sans

One serif for titles and one sans for everything else, in light and dark themes you can toggle with Ctrl J.

Tuned to your device

On your first visit, Time12 asks what you are using. It also reads reduced-motion and data-saver settings and drops to a lighter mode on its own if frames run slow. Switch any time from the button beside the theme toggle.

Common questions

Who creates tasks, groups and accounts?

The organization account, meaning whoever registers. It adds groups, sub-groups and member accounts. Member accounts do not see the Add button in the Org tab.

What can a member hand in?

Text, links and files, in any combination. Files can be up to 25 MB each. At least one of the three is required.

What happens when a submission is rejected?

The task goes back to the member as Rejected, with your reason. They can resubmit until the deadline, and every attempt stays in the history as its own version.

What happens after the deadline?

A task nobody handed in is marked overdue, and the member sees Deadline Passed — Submissions Closed. Analytics counts it as missed.

Can I export results?

Yes. The Analytics tab has a Download Excel button for the current view: the whole organization, a group, a person or a single task.

Do I need an email address to sign up?

No. You register with a username and a password. Accounts you create for members use the same, named under your organization.

What is an Automated Task?

You write what to check for and a pass mark. The AI marks each submission against it, accepts work that clears the mark and sends the rest back with feedback. Only the organization owner can create one.

What are papers?

You earn a paper for each task that is verified or accepted by the AI, and lose one for each task that closes unfinished. They are counted from the real task record and shown in the Rewards tab.

Can someone other than the owner run a group?

Yes. Make them an admin of a group. They can send assignments, add and remove members and watch progress for that group and its sub-groups, and nothing outside them.

Does it work offline?

Notes do. They are saved on your device first and sync when you are back online. Tasks and submissions need a connection.
