The Epic

The Ramayana is one of the two great Sanskrit epics of ancient India, traditionally attributed to Maharishi Valmiki. What follows is the traditional narrative arc of the epic. Events marked Part One are confirmed to appear in Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part One; the rest belong to the wider epic and may be depicted in Part Two or not filmed at all. Nothing here is invented dialogue or scene detail.

  1. Part One

    The Kingdom of Ayodhya

    King Dasharatha of Ayodhya, childless for years, performs the Putrakameshti yagna. Rama is born to Queen Kaushalya, with Bharata, Lakshmana and Shatrughna born soon after to Kaikeyi and Sumitra.

  2. Part One

    Childhood & Training

    The four princes are raised and trained in Ayodhya under the guidance of Sage Vashishtha, learning statecraft, scripture and the arts of war.

  3. Part One

    Sage Vishwamitra's Call

    Sage Vishwamitra arrives at Ayodhya asking for Rama's protection against demons harassing his hermitage. Rama and Lakshmana leave with him, beginning their first trials.

  4. Part One

    Mithila & the Swayamvara

    In Mithila, Rama meets Sita, daughter of King Janaka, and wins her hand by stringing and breaking the divine bow of Shiva at her swayamvara.

  5. Part One

    Kaikeyi's Two Boons

    On the eve of Rama's coronation, Manthara persuades Kaikeyi to claim two boons once promised by Dasharatha: Bharata's coronation, and Rama's exile for fourteen years.

  6. Part One

    The Fourteen-Year Exile

    Rama accepts the exile without protest. Sita and Lakshmana insist on accompanying him, and the three leave Ayodhya's comforts for the forest.

  7. Life in the Forest

    The three live an austere life moving between hermitages, eventually settling at Panchavati in the Dandaka forest, encountering sages and forest-dwellers along the way.

  8. Surpanakha's Encounter

    Ravana's sister Surpanakha approaches Rama and is rebuffed; in the confrontation that follows, Lakshmana wounds her, and she flees to Lanka seeking revenge.

  9. The Golden Deer

    Ravana sends the demon Maricha, disguised as a golden deer, to lure Rama away from the hermitage, drawing Lakshmana away as well and leaving Sita alone.

  10. Sita's Abduction

    Disguised as a mendicant, Ravana abducts Sita and carries her to Lanka. The vulture Jatayu intervenes to protect her and is mortally wounded by Ravana.

  11. The Search Begins

    Rama and Lakshmana search the forests for Sita, meeting the dying Jatayu, and eventually forming an alliance with Sugriva, the exiled king of the vanaras of Kishkindha.

  12. Hanuman's Devotion

    Hanuman, minister to Sugriva, becomes Rama's most devoted follower. He leaps across the ocean to Lanka, finds Sita in captivity, and returns with news of her.

  13. The War in Lanka

    The vanara army builds a bridge to Lanka. After a prolonged war, Rama confronts and defeats Ravana, securing the triumph of dharma over adharma.

About the Film

Ramayana is a two-part Hindi-language mythological epic directed by Nitesh Tiwari and produced by Namit Malhotra. Part One released worldwide on November 6, 2026; Part Two is slated for Diwali 2027.

Nitesh Tiwari — Director

An Indian filmmaker best known for directing Dangal (2016) and Chhichhore (2019). He co-wrote Ramayana's screenplay and directs both parts of the adaptation.

Namit Malhotra — Producer

Indian entrepreneur and founder of Prime Focus, and chairman and group chief executive of DNEG. Ramayana is produced through Prime Focus Studios, bringing together global VFX infrastructure for the production.

Yash — Co-Producer & Actor

Indian actor known for the K.G.F. film series, who plays Ravana and co-produces the film through his banner, Monster Mind Creations.
